# Design Reference
## Purpose
Research 3–8 relevant high-quality interfaces and extract reusable principles without cloning brand identity.
## Analyze
- Information architecture and navigation
- Grid, max-width, density, section rhythm and whitespace
- Typography hierarchy and measure
- Semantic color strategy, borders, elevation and radius
- Forms, tables, cards, dialogs, search, filters and CTA patterns
- Hover/focus/loading/empty/error states
- Motion purpose and duration
- Desktop/tablet/mobile behavior
## Selection
Prefer references from the same product category and audience. Mix references when useful rather than imitating a single site.
## Output
Create a short reference matrix: pattern observed → why it works → whether to adopt/adapt/reject.
## Prohibited
Do not copy logos, proprietary illustrations, text, distinctive brand assets, or an entire page pixel-for-pixel.
